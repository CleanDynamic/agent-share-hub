# NeoScale AI — Project Knowledge

## BRAND

- **Platform name:** NeoScale AI
- **Tagline:** The AI Agent Tactics Forum. For Builders and Normies.
- **Primary colour:** `#E8571A` (orange) — use for primary buttons, badges, accents
- **Secondary colour:** `#2EC4B6` (teal) — use for secondary actions and tags
- **Background:** `#0A0A0F` (near black) — page background
- **Surface:** `#111118` — card and panel background
- **Border:** `#1E1E2A` — subtle borders
- **Text primary:** `#FFFFFF`
- **Text secondary:** `#9999AA`
- **Font:** Inter or system sans-serif

## STYLE RULES

- Dark mode only. No light mode.
- Cards use subtle border (`#1E1E2A`), rounded corners (12px), no drop shadows.
- Buttons: primary = orange fill, secondary = teal outline.
- All body text must be legible at small sizes against dark backgrounds.
- Design is bold, technical, accessible. Not corporate. Not cute.

## PLATFORM RULES

- All content is AI-agnostic — works with ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Grok, or any AI tool.
- Every content card must display: content type badge, AI tools it works with, difficulty level.
- All user-facing descriptions must be written in plain English a non-technical person understands.
- **Never** use these words in user-facing copy: agent, agentic, LLM, RAG, pipeline, system prompt.
- **Use instead:** AI assistant, ready-made setup, automation, connect your apps.
