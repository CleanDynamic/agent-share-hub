## Two-wayPairs that can run at the same time
The drop check
RefusedA different build, an unknown version, or no tiles placed at all.
Fixed and listedColumns that don't add up to 12 are scaled. A tile listed twice keeps its first place.
Fixed and listedTiles from two sessions in one box are split into one box per session.
Fixed and listedA box above a tile it depends on moves down to the first row where order holds.
Fixed and listedTwo-way pairs that depend on each other are removed. Ends that aren't level slide into the same band.
Left out and listedTiles not in the file stay unplaced in Sources. Lines written by the chat are ignored: lines come from the build.
