Gutters (tinted) are the only place lines run. Three links share the lower gutter at an even spacing.
✓Routed for you: out of a tile's edge, through gutters, into the other tile's edge. Never across a box.
✓Several lines in one gutter run side by side, evenly spaced.
✓Drag a line's middle segment to another gutter. It keeps its right angles and snaps to the gutter's centre.
✓Moving a box re-routes its lines at once.
✓Checks get a dashed line to what they test. Two-way arrows are mint and always level.
✕No line can be drawn between tiles with no link, so the map never claims a dependency the build doesn't have.
